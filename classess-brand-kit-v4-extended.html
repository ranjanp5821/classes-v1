Accordions

Serif questions, a hairline divider per row, and a paint edge that lights the open item. Used for FAQs, lesson outlines, and settings groups.

What does an engine actually generate?
A complete, reviewable lesson for one topic — scenario, worked steps, an interactive, and a practice set — keyed to the subject's colour and ready for a teacher to approve.
Can a school keep its own branding?
The wordmark stays monochrome, but the paint layer flexes — a school can run its house colour across its portal without breaking the system.
How is content kept accurate?
Every generated answer passes a verify gate before it can be published. Anything that fails is flagged for a human check rather than shipped.
